## 🧠 The Arithmo-Edge Quad-Phase Resilience Theory (`QPRT-4`)

**Arithmo-Edge** (*Powered by Olympiad Edge*) operates high-stakes, real-time mathematical competitions across every global UTC timezone—frequently while human headquarters staff in Dhaka (`03:00 BST`) are asleep. To eliminate single points of failure, rating corruption, and unfair submission penalties, every contest, matchmaking queue, and billing lifecycle is governed by **The Arithmo-Edge Quad-Phase Resilience Theory (`QPRT-4`)**:

* **Phase I — `PRE-EMPTIVE CONTAINMENT` (Zero-Trust Pre-Flight Gates):**
  * Eliminates predictable failure vectors *before* the contest clock starts (`PlagioGuard AI` similarity scanning, `IndexedDB` encrypted payload pre-caching, `Atomic UTC` header locking, and `Golden Vault` standby problem seeding).
* **Phase II — `AUTONOMOUS INTERCEPTION` (Sub-Second Telemetry Watchdogs):**
  * Continuous `10-second` Cloudflare-to-Firebase heartbeat monitors, solve-velocity anomaly detectors, ASN regional drop classifiers, and `0%` AC submission surge triggers isolate faults before widespread damage occurs.
* **Phase III — `RAPID SURGICAL RELIEF` (30s Hot-Swaps, 2-Min Rejudges & Edge Fallbacks):**
  * Executes automated, deterministic remediation without waiting for human intervention—hot-swapping leaked problems in `30 seconds`, running symbolic SymPy global rejudges in `2 minutes`, freezing global clocks on infrastructure stalls, and switching Spectator endpoints to `60s` Edge CDN snapshots.
* **Phase IV — `ASYMMETRIC FAIR-PLAY COMPENSATION` (Zero-Dampening ELO Shield):**
  * Enforces the **Zero-Dampening ELO Guarantee**: no honest competitor ever loses ELO, forfeits a strict `Max 2` Expert submission slot, or suffers punitive anti-cheat flags due to platform, judge, or regional infrastructure anomalies.

### 🛡️ Dropdown 1: Edge Case #1 — Direct AoPS Wiki Leak & Rapid Reputation Shield (`4-Step Protocol`)

<details>
<summary><b>Click to Expand Full Protocol: Pre-Contest PlagioGuard Gate, 30s Golden Vault Hot-Swap, +3 Bounty ELO & Blacklist</b></summary>

<br />

* **Threat Profile & Operational Impact:**
  * A community-contributed or third-party contest problem is discovered verbatim—or under trivial algebraic disguise—on **Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) Wiki**, national Olympiad archives, or public forums before or during a live rated round, enabling bad actors to copy official solutions in seconds.

* **Step 1 — Pre-Contest `Zero-AoPS Similarity Gate` (`Arithmo PlagioGuard AI`):**
  * **Trigger:** Every candidate problem enters `Arithmo Forge` staging prior to contest lock.
  * **Automated Execution:** `PlagioGuard AI` extracts structural LaTeX ASTs, geometric constraint graphs, number-theoretic congruences, and semantic embeddings, cross-indexing them against mirrored AoPS Wiki corpora and historical Olympiad databases.
  * **Hard Block Threshold:** Any problem exceeding **`75%` structural or semantic similarity** is automatically rejected, quarantined from the live pool, and forced through parameter/bound mutation and re-verification before eligibility is reconsidered.

* **Step 2 — Live `30-Second` Standby Problem Hot-Swap (`Arithmo PulseWatcher AI`):**
  * **Trigger:** Either a competitor clicks the in-arena **`Direct AoPS Leak`** integrity button and submits a verifiable AoPS URL, **or** `PulseWatcher AI` detects an anomalous sub-`30s` solve-velocity spike (`>4σ` above expected difficulty baseline) on a single problem.
  * **Automated Execution (`≤ 30 Seconds`):**
    * The compromised item is immediately locked and flagged **`LEAK-FLAGGED`** across all active clients.
    * `NightWarden AI` (or an active duty officer on `Subsite #12: Headquarters Core`) pulls a pre-verified **Standby Reserve Problem (`Problem A2`)** from the **Co-Founders' Golden Vault** (which maintains **`2` sealed, SymPy-verified backups per difficulty segment**).
    * Clients hot-swap the active problem card seamlessly without page reload and automatically append **`+10 minutes` of global bonus time** to the round clock.

* **Step 3 — `Asymmetric Fair-Play ELO Shield` & Whistleblower Bounty:**
  * **Honest Reporter Reward:** The first competitor who reports a verified AoPS leak link rather than submitting a copied answer receives an immediate **`+3 Contribution ELO`** bounty and an *Integrity Sentinel* ledger badge.
  * **Scoreboard & Rating Neutralization:** All points, time penalties, and `Max 2` submission counts recorded on the leaked problem are wiped clean.
  * **Multi-Leak Round Safeguard:** If `2` or more problems in the same round are confirmed leaked, the entire round automatically converts to **`Unrated for Positive Gain`**—preventing cheaters from farming ELO while guaranteeing honest participants lose `0` ELO.

* **Step 4 — Permanent `Plagiarism Blacklist`:**
  * **Enforcement:** Any community problemsetter whose accepted problem is proven to be a copied AoPS/Olympiad problem suffers permanent revocation of `Arithmo Forge` problemsetting privileges, immediate forfeiture of all previously earned `Contribution ELO`, and public audit logging on `Subsite #12`.

### ⚖️ Dropdown 2: Edge Case #2 — Heuristic Flaws, Unsolvable Questions & Wrong-Answer Judge Key Bugs (`4-Step Protocol`)

<details>
<summary><b>Click to Expand Full Protocol: Automated Question Quarantine, 2-Min SymPy Global Rejudge & Zero-Dampening Rollback</b></summary>

<br />

* **Threat Profile & Operational Impact:**
  * A problem contains an ill-posed constraint (heuristic flaw yielding zero or multiple valid answers) or an inverted/typoed judge answer key that rejects mathematically valid solutions—threatening competitors' strict **`Max 2 Submissions`** cap in Expert divisions and corrupting ELO deltas.

* **Step 1 — Automated `Question Quarantine` (`Arithmo PulseWatcher AI`):**
  * **Trigger:** Activated instantaneously if **`10+` competitors submit answers with `0%` acceptance rate** on a problem, **or** if **`2+` rated competitors (`2000+ ELO`)** file an *Errata / Judge Key Dispute* flag.
  * **Automated Execution:**
    * The problem transitions immediately into **`QUARANTINED (Under Automated Audit)`**.
    * **Submission Counter Freeze:** Every competitor's `Max 2` submission counter for that problem is immediately frozen in place so no participant burns their remaining attempt against a faulty judge.

* **Step 2 — `2-Minute` `AxiomCheck AI + Python/SymPy` Audit & Instant Global Rejudge:**
  * **Automated Verification (`≤ 120 Seconds`):** `AxiomCheck AI` synthesizes an independent symbolic and numerical verification script using **Python + SymPy** (and deterministic brute-force bounds checking) from the raw problem statement, cross-checking the stored `Answer Key` against all submitted competitor payloads.
  * **Branch A — Judge Key Mismatch (Valid Answer Rejected):**
    * `AxiomCheck AI` patches the canonical `Answer Key` automatically and triggers an **Instant Global Rejudge** within **`2 minutes`**.
    * Every affected `Wrong Answer (WA)` verdict flips cleanly to **`Accepted (AC)`**, wasted `Max 2` submission slots are restored, and false wrong-attempt time penalties are erased.
  * **Branch B — Heuristic / Unsolvable Flaw (Ill-Posed Problem):**
    * The problem is permanently marked **`VOIDED (Question Cancelled)`** and removed from scoreboard weightings.
    * All submission counters on the voided item are reset to `0`, and cumulative time spent/penalized on that question is stripped from tie-breaker calculations.

* **Step 3 — `Zero-Dampening ELO Rollback & Freeze`:**
  * **Rating Pipeline Hold:** The ELO settlement engine holds final rating commits until all `AxiomCheck AI` rejudges reach `100%` completion.
  * **Asymmetric Delta Protection:** If a voided question materially distorted time allocation across the round, the round switches to **`Unrated for Negative Delta`**—verified top performers retain their positive ELO gains, while every competitor with a negative delta is clamped to **`0` ELO change**.

* **Step 4 — `Subsite #12` 1-Click HQ Rejudge Command:**
  * **Human-in-the-Loop Override:** Any *Round Coordinator* or *Prime Sovereign* logged into **`Subsite #12 (Headquarters Core)`** retains a dedicated **1-Click HQ Rejudge / Hot-Patch / Void Console** to override, inspect SymPy proof logs, or re-run batch evaluations at any moment.

### 🌙 Dropdown 3: Edge Case #3 — Midnight Server Outages, Traffic Spikes & Browser Disconnections (`4-Step Protocol`)

<details>
<summary><b>Click to Expand Full Protocol: 10s Heartbeat Auto-Freeze, IndexedDB Offline Vault & NightWarden Crisis Compensation</b></summary>

<br />

* **Threat Profile & Operational Impact:**
  * Upstream cloud latency, database lockups, or client Wi-Fi drops occur at `03:00 BST` during high-stakes rated rounds or sponsored prize events while human operators are offline.

* **Step 1 — `Auto-Freeze & Time-Extension Watchdog` (`Arithmo GridShield AI`):**
  * **Continuous Probe:** Cloudflare Edge Workers and Firebase Realtime/Firestore exchange an authenticated **Heartbeat Ping every `10 seconds`**.
  * **Trigger & Freeze (`30s` Threshold):** If database writes or API endpoints fail `3` consecutive heartbeats (**`30 consecutive seconds`**), `GridShield AI` immediately shifts the active contest state into **`FROZEN (Technical Pause)`**.
  * **Exact Time-Extension:** Clients display a calm, non-alarmist telemetry banner pausing local countdowns; the exact millisecond duration of the outage is recorded and automatically appended as an **`Auto Time-Extension`** upon service recovery.

* **Step 2 — `Offline Local Vault + Auto-Sync` (`IndexedDB` Capsule):**
  * **Pre-Match Encryption:** At contest start (`T - 00:00`), encrypted problem payloads and validation salts are persisted into the browser's local **`IndexedDB` Capsule**.
  * **Zero-Interruption Solving:** If a competitor's local ISP drops mid-contest, the arena remains fully readable and interactive offline. Competitors enter and lock their answers locally; each submission is sealed with an offline cryptographic state hash and **auto-synced** to the edge queue the instant connectivity resumes.

* **Step 3 — `Arithmo NightWarden AI` Autonomous Crisis Warden:**
  * **Trigger:** If **`>20%` of active contest participants** emit client-side submission timeout errors during unstaffed hours (`00:00 – 07:00 BST`).
  * **Automated Relief Actions:**
    * `NightWarden AI` publishes a signed, timestamped official incident bulletin directly into the live arena feed.
    * Activates **`Safe-Rollback / Unrated`** ELO protection if server instability cannot be resolved within `10 minutes`.
    * Automatically credits a free **Compensation Rematch Pass** directly into affected participants' **`Arithmo Wallets`** for any paid or sponsored tournament.

* **Step 4 — `Multi-Timezone Sentinel Rotation`:**
  * **24/7 Operational Coverage:** Distributes `Chief Arbiter` and `Round Coordinator` on-call shifts across **Americas (UTC-5)**, **Europe (UTC+0/+1)**, and **Asia (UTC+6)** rosters inside `Subsite #12`, ensuring human escalation is always awake alongside `NightWarden AI`.

</details>

### 🌍 Dropdown 4: Edge Cases #4 – #7 — Regional Internet Blackouts, Clock Drift, Notation Ambiguity & Quota Spikes

<details>
<summary><b>Click to Expand Full Protocols: ASN Geo-Outage Amnesty, Edge Atomic UTC, SymPy Canonicalizer & Edge Read-Shield</b></summary>

<br />

* **Edge Case #4 — Regional Submarine Cable / Country-Wide Internet Outage (`Geo-Outage Amnesty`):**
  * **Trigger:** `GridShield AI` telemetry detects a sudden **`>60%` simultaneous drop** in active client heartbeats originating from a specific country code or Autonomous System Number (ASN—e.g., nationwide submarine cable cut or ISP blackout in Bangladesh or India) while global edge health remains `>99.5%`.
  * **Automated Rapid Relief:**
    * `GridShield AI` isolates all competitor sessions bound to the affected ASN/country prefix.
    * Applies **Geo-Outage Rating Amnesty**: any regional competitor disconnected mid-round has their match participation automatically converted to **`Unrated`**, guaranteeing that regional infrastructure blackouts never destroy hard-earned ELO while unaffected regions continue uninterrupted.

* **Edge Case #5 — Client Device Clock Drift & Millisecond Tie-Breaker Manipulation:**
  * **Trigger:** Competitors operating on misconfigured OS system clocks (BIOS battery drift, timezone misalignments) or attempting local `Date.now()` clock tampering to falsify faster solve-time tie-breakers.
  * **Automated Rapid Relief:**
    * Local device clocks are strictly ignored for all scoring, deadline enforcement, and tie-breaker calculations.
    * Every contest timer and submission timestamp synchronizes exclusively against **Cloudflare Edge Atomic UTC Headers (`X-Edge-Server-Time`)**, enforced server-side at the edge worker ingress before Firestore persistence.

* **Edge Case #6 — Equivalent Mathematical Notation Formatting Disputes (`1/2` vs `0.5` vs `2^-1`):**
  * **Trigger:** A competitor submits a mathematically exact, valid answer in an alternate representation (such as `0.5`, `3/6`, `2^-1`, or `\frac{1}{2}`) that fails a naive literal string comparison against `1/2`.
  * **Automated Rapid Relief:**
    * Before any `Wrong Answer` verdict or `Max 2` submission deduction can be finalized, **`Arithmo AxiomCheck AI`** routes the payload through its **Symbolic Canonicalizer (`SymPy` algebraic & rational equivalence engine)**.
    * Any reduced fraction, exact decimal, or algebraically equivalent expression is automatically accepted as **`AC`**, unless the problem explicitly enforces a strict formatting constraint (e.g., *"Give your answer modulo $10^9 + 7$"* or *"Express as a single reduced integer"*).

* **Edge Case #7 — Sudden Viral Traffic Spike Exhausting Free Firebase Quota (`Edge Read-Shield`):**
  * **Trigger:** Viral social traffic or `10,000+` concurrent spectators flood **`Subsite #7` (Live Standings)** or **`Subsite #6`** simultaneously, threatening to exhaust Firebase's daily free-tier document read quota and starve live competitors.
  * **Automated Rapid Relief:**
    * `GridShield AI` immediately engages **Aggressive Edge CDN Caching** (`60-second` TTL on Cloudflare Workers paired with client `LocalStorage` Stale-While-Revalidate).
    * Public spectators are served ultra-fast, static JSON leaderboard snapshots directly from Cloudflare's global edge cache—consuming **zero Firestore reads**—while **`100%` of live Firestore read/write throughput** is strictly reserved for active competing athletes.

  * **Trigger:** An opponent abruptly closes their browser tab, kills their WebSocket connection, or abandons the arena after falling behind in a rated **1v1 Bullet Blitz** duel.

* **Edge Case #10 — Payment Webhook Delay During Academy Renewal Ultimatum (`5-Day Grace Buffer`):**
  * **Trigger:** Upstream payment processor (**Lemon Squeezy / Paddle**) webhook delivery is delayed by banking or queue latency during the final hour of an academy's **5-Day Renewal Grace Period** or **3-Day Guidebook Grace Period**.
  * **Automated Rapid Relief:**

* **Edge Case #11 — External LLM API Outage (`Gemini / Qwen-Math` Downtime):**
