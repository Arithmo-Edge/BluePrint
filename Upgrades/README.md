### Ecosystem Architectural Principles
- **Zero-Fragmentation Olympiad Philosophy:** Instead of scattering users across 40–60 isolated micro-sites, **Arithmo-Edge** unifies competitive mathematics into **4 Core IMO Hubs** (*Number Theory, Algebra, Combinatorics, Geometry*) powered by **Dynamic Filterable Olympiad Tags**, **AI Blog-to-Lab Curation**, and **Dual Community Guidebooks**.
- **Sensory Cyber-Olympiad Immersion:** Combines **Three.js (WebGL)** prime constellations, **GSAP & Framer Motion** physics transitions, **Glassmorphism** dark neon UI, and zero-asset **Web Audio API** tactile synthesis into an elite engineering standard.

<details>
<summary><b>📐 4 Core IMO Hubs & Dynamic Olympiad Tagging System (`Zero-Fragmentation Architecture`)</b></summary>
<br>

* **1. The 4 Master Olympiad Branches (`V11.0+` Unification):**
  * **Hub 01 — `Number Theory Hub`:** Unifies the foundational 5 arenas (*Prime Factors, GCD-LCM, Number of Divisors $\tau(N)$, Square Root, Sum of Divisors $\sigma(N)$*) alongside Modular Arithmetic, Diophantine Equations, Euler Totient $\phi(N)$, Quadratic Residues, and $p$-adic Valuations.

  * **Hub 04 — `Geometry Hub`:** Covers Triangle Centers & Cevians, Circle Theorems & Power of a Point, Radical Axes, Homothety, Complex/Barycentric Coordinates, and Projective Geometry.

  * Sub-topics operate as filterable tags (`#Modular-Arithmetic`, `#Pigeonhole`, `#Vieta-Jumping`, `#Radical-Axis`) rather than separate websites—preserving **Mixed Olympiad-Style Contests** where multiple branches fuse together and concentrating thousands of competitors in shared arenas.
