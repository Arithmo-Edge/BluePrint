### Strict Operational Division of Labor
- **HQ-Only `@handle` Authentication Gate:** Subsite #12 is completely closed to the public and regular users. Only verified members holding approved official **`@handle`** credentials and active Firebase session tokens can pass the gate.
- **Problem Authoring & Sandbox Testing (`Subsite #10: Arithmo Forge`):** When HQ members (*Problem Architects, Core Sentinels, Chief Mathematical Architect*) author problems for **Sunday Mega Arena Contests**, **Saturday Special Episodes**, or **Academy Olympiads**, they execute all sandbox compilation, testcase generation, and editorial drafting inside the `Arithmo Forge` studio.

## 2. Expanded Headquarters Architecture & Governance

<details>
<summary><b>Dropdown 1: The 5-Tier Monotonic HQ ELO Ladder & Strict Positional Chronology</b> (Click to expand)</summary>

### Monotonic Non-Decreasing HQ ELO Rule
Unlike competitive arena ELOs where ratings fluctuate dynamically based on wins and losses, **HQ ELO NEVER decreases**. It grows monotonically through verified governance deeds: answering junior Q&A threads, auditing problemsets, mentoring `Core Sentinels`, curating guidebooks, and maintaining platform integrity.

### Strict Positional Chronology
A lower-ranked HQ member **never overtakes** a higher-ranked member on the HQ standing board even if their raw HQ ELO temporarily surpasses the senior member's score. Rank advancement occurs strictly upon formal tier promotion issued via signed Sovereign certificates.

### Dual Promotion Requirement
Advancing to any higher HQ tier requires **BOTH** reaching the target HQ ELO bracket **AND** being issued an exact **Prime Sovereign Certificate**.

### The 5 Official Headquarters Tiers
*   **1. Core Sentinel (`Starting HQ ELO: 100`)**
    *   *Role:* Entry-level governance auditors and test-solvers.
    *   *Responsibilities:* Audit candidate problemsets from the Forge, assist junior learners in discussion channels, and monitor initial telemetry queues.
*   **2. Problem Architect (`Starting HQ ELO: 300`)**
    *   *Role:* Senior Olympiad problem authors and curators.
    *   *Responsibilities:* Author rounds in `Arithmo Forge`, review Guidebook theory additions (`+2 Contribution ELO`), and calibrate segment difficulties.
*   **3. Round Coordinator (`Starting HQ ELO: 500`)**
    *   *Role:* Live competition directors.
    *   *Responsibilities:* Schedule daily/Saturday/Sunday Mega rounds, configure `2` standby backup problems per segment, and trigger `1-Click Problem Hot-Swaps` or `Global Rejudges`.
*   **4. Chief Arbiter (`Starting HQ ELO: 750`)**
    *   *Role:* Supreme judicial officers.
    *   *Responsibilities:* Review `HonestyScore AI` forensic packets, conduct `5-Minute Live Skill Audits`, issue final appeal verdicts, and rotate across global timezones (`Multi-Timezone Sentinel Rotation`).
*   **5. Prime Sovereign (`Starting HQ ELO: 1000` — Top 3 Founders/Leaders • Flag-Immune)**
    *   *Role:* Highest executive command tier.
    *   *Responsibilities:* Holds **Flag-Immune** status against automated false flags, issues **Prime Sovereign Certificates** for HQ inductions and promotions, approves `Honorary Prime Sovereign` honors, and controls the **Emergency HQ Circuit Breaker**.
