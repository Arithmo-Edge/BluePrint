- **Centralized `Arithmo-Core-SDK` Updates:** All anti-cheat detection thresholds, gaze tolerance windows, and security heuristics deploy globally across all 13 integrated ecosystem sites via a single, immutable script update (`engine.js`).

<details>
<summary><b>📂 Dropdown 1: Layer 1 & 2 — Module 7 Optical/Audio Proctoring & MBTS Telemetry Clustering</b></summary>

<br>

* **1. Site-Wide Hardware Gate & Unified Consent (`engine.html`):**
  * Pre-flight webcam and microphone diagnostic checks execute prior to match entry.
  * Persistent hardware permission checks run upon login: blocking camera or microphone access allows unrated Gym, Problemset, and Social access, but displays a prominent banner reading **`Rated Arena Locked — Enable Camera & Mic to Compete`** across all rated contests and rated `1v1` matches.
  * Denying camera/mic consent during a legacy rated entry deducts an automatic **`-10 ELO`**.
  * **Exemptions:** Camera and microphone remain inactive during unrated practice sessions; audio collusion flags are fully disabled during collaborative **Team Contests**.

* **2. Optical Telemetry & `4.5s` Context-Aware Adaptive Gaze Guard:**
  * The standard off-screen gaze buffer is set to **`2.0 seconds`**, which **automatically widens to `4.5 seconds`** whenever a complex multi-factor problem (`N ≥ 3` prime factors) is rendered on screen or typing pauses (`Idle`) to allow for genuine mental calculation.
  * Slightly exceeding the buffer triggers only a non-punitive yellow **`GAZE DEFLECTION`** HUD warning with **zero ELO loss**.
  * **Hard Camera Strike Triggers:** Leaving the seat or exiting the camera frame entirely, intentionally covering the camera lens or plunging the room into darkness, or detecting **multiple human faces/heads** inside the camera frame.

* **3. 3-Axis Neural Audio Collusion Trap (Zero Ambient False Positives):**
  * Intelligent filtering ignores household background noises such as ceiling fans, traffic, and distant chatter; triggers an audio collusion flag **ONLY when all 3 conditions occur simultaneously**:
    1. Clear human speech or whispering (`Voice Activity`) is detected on the microphone.
    2. Competitor gaze deflects off-screen or toward a secondary device at that exact precise moment.
    3. Competitor types the exact answer at superhuman speed (**`100ms – 2500ms`**) with **zero backspaces** immediately following the cessation of speech.

* **4. Layer 2 — `MBTS` (Measure of Behavioral & Telemetry Similarity):**
  * Executes during the **`30-minute`** post-General and **`1-hour`** post-Expert processing windows.
  * Ignores trivial problems and compares identical wrong answers, factorization entry sequences, keystroke timing intervals, and network latency signals on complex problems to detect coordinated cheating rings or automated solver bots (treating registered team members as a single unified entity in Team Contests).

* **5. Layer 3 — AI Moderation & Button Firewall (`V7.0 – V10.0`):**
  * Hard-locks the `Regenerate` button and helper lifelines during rated matches, locks `Arithmo Personal AI` and `Arithmo Guild AI` during live rated contests, and runs pre-post AI toxicity and leak moderation across all blogs, Q&A boards, and `@handle` DMs.

* **Step 1 — `Flags 1 & 2` (`1 Plus Contest Lockdown`):** Immediate **`-5 ELO`** deduction combined with a **`1 Plus Contest Lockdown`** (the next rated match where the user achieves a positive rating gain `ΔR > 0` has its entire gain nullified to `0 ELO` to clear the security lock).
* **Step 2 — `Flags 3 through 8` (`2 Plus Contest Lockdown`):** Escalates to nullifying positive ELO gains across the **next `2` positive rated matches** (`0 ELO` net gain); additional infractions incurred during active lockdown extend the counter.

* **Step 4 — `Flag 10` (`1-Month Temporary Ban`):** Mandatory **`30-day` temporary suspension** applied across all rated arenas and social features, followed by a mandatory hardware security hard-reboot upon returning.
* **Step 5 — `Flag 11` (`Permanent Gmail & IP/Device Ban`):** Any infraction committed after returning from the 30-day temporary ban triggers a **permanent irreversible ban** enforced at two distinct security tiers:
  * **Permanent Gmail Blacklist:** Blocks the Google OAuth 2.0 email address across all 13 ecosystem websites forever.
  * **IP Address & Browser Hardware Fingerprint Block:** Blocks the network IP address and hashed browser device fingerprint to prevent alt-account re-entry.
