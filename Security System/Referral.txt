## 9. Multi-Motivator Referral & Contribution ELO System
To drive organic Olympiad community growth without spam or self-referral abuse, implement a **Mutual Handshake Referral Verification** protocol:

1. **Eligibility & Multi-Motivator Rules:**
   - **7-Day New User Window:** An inviter can only send a referral request to a target `@handle` whose account was created within the **last 7 days**.
   - **Active Contest Proof:** The invitee must have already solved **at least 1 problem in 1 Rated Contest** before a request can be sent.
   - **Up to 3 Co-Motivators:** A new user can confirm (`"Yes"`) referral requests from up to **3 different inviters** (recognizing that multiple peers may motivate a student to join).
   - **Device & Anti-Loop Guardrails:**
     - `inviterDeviceHash` and `inviteeDeviceHash` (via browser fingerprinting) MUST be distinct.
     - No restrictions on account creation order (inviter may join after invitee) or shared Academy/Private Room membership.
     - **One-Time Request Lock:** Once `@userA` sends a request to `@userB` (whether confirmed or rejected), `@userA` can never request `@userB` again.
     - **No Mutual Swap ($A \leftrightarrow B$):** If `@userA` already confirmed `@userB`'s invite, `@userB` cannot send an invite request to `@userA`.

2. **Weekly Rate-Limit & Paid Expansion Pack:**
   - **Free Weekly Quota:** Each `@handle` can send up to **7 free invite requests per 7-day rolling week** (no lifetime cap).
   - **Extra Invite Pack (`$5.00 = +10 Invites`):** Users can purchase **+10 additional invite slots** anytime via their closed-loop **Arithmo Wallet**.

3. **Two-Step Irreversible Confirmation UX:**
   - **Sending Request:** Inviter enters the friend's `@handle` on their profile page. Creates a `PENDING` request valid for **7 days** and generates an auth-locked deep link (`https://arithmoedge.com{requestId}`).
   - **Homepage & Deep-Link Modal:** Whenever the logged-in invitee visits the homepage (or opens their auth-locked deep link), show a non-blocking modal: *"@inviter claims they invited you to Arithmo-Edge. Is this true?"* with **`[ হ্যাঁ (Yes) ]`**, **`[ না (No) ]`**, and **`[ Skip for now ]`**.
   - **Persistent Reminder & Final Lock:** Clicking `Skip for now` dismisses the modal temporarily until the next homepage visit. Clicking `Yes` or `No` triggers a second confirmation dialog (*"Are you sure? This decision is permanent and cannot be changed."*). Confirming permanently locks the status (`CONFIRMED` or `REJECTED`).
