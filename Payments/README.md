## 🧭 1. Economic Philosophy & Dual-Wallet Topology

**Arithmo-Edge** (*Powered by Olympiad Edge*) enforces a strict architectural wall between **competitive merit** and **monetized utility**. Every financial flow across the ecosystem is governed by three foundational axioms:

* **Zero Pay-to-Win Competitive Integrity:** Financial spend **never** buys ELO rating points, extra contest clock time, hint injections during live rated rounds, or automated judge leniency. Every daily rated contest and Sunday Mega Arena Contest is **100% free** to enter for every registered user worldwide. Subscriptions and Pay-As-You-Go packs strictly unlock AI mentorship depth (`General 1x` vs. `Premium 2x`), private practice rooms, direct friend duels, custom contest hosting, and institutional academy management tooling.
* **Complete Segregation of Dual Wallets:**
  * **1. Personal `Arithmo Wallet` (`Arithmo-Edge Core Portal`):** An individual consumer ledger owned and operated exclusively by a single learner. Supports `+10%` top-up bonus bundles, direct **`@handle` Pass Gifting**, in-app monthly academy membership dues settlement, and automated merit credit grants at **`+50`, `+100`, `+250`, and `+500` `Contribution ELO`** milestones.
  * **2. Institutional `Academy Wallet` (`Inside Subsite #9: Arithmo Academy Arena`):** A completely isolated institutional treasury ledger operated **strictly by the Academy Admin Panel** (`Min 1 – Max 5 Admins`; `Max 10 Admins` in `Academy Enterprise`). Standard academy members and mentors have zero visibility, spend authority, or top-up permissions over `Academy Wallet` funds.
* **Closed-Loop Digital Credit & Merchant of Record (`Lemon Squeezy / Paddle`):** All wallet balances operate exclusively as **closed-loop, non-cash digital platform credits** (`Strictly No Cash-Out / No Fiat Withdrawal`), legally insulating **Arithmo-Edge** from gambling or money-transmitter classifications. Global checkout, card processing, and international VAT/sales-tax remittance are handled end-to-end via **Lemon Squeezy / Paddle** Merchant-of-Record webhooks.
